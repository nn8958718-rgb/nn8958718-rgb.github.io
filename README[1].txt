HOW THE GIFT WORKS
1. A black screen appears with a glowing heart. Tap it.
2. The decorated cake page appears. Tap either candle.
3. “Happy Birthday Mimo” appears. Tap “A little surprise for you”.
4. Your letter page appears. Add your personal letter image as letter.png in this same folder.
5. Tap “One last thing…” to reach the 19 birthday candles.
6. Tap the 19 candles to reveal the final message.

YOUR LETTER
The letter page is ready to display your own letter image. Save your letter picture as exactly `letter.png` and put it beside index.html, style.css, and script.js. The website currently shows a decorated placeholder until letter.png is added.

PUBLISH ON GITHUB PAGES
1. Upload index.html, style.css, and script.js to the root of your GitHub repository.
2. Upload letter.png to the same root folder after adding your letter picture.
3. Open Settings > Pages.
4. Under Build and deployment, choose Deploy from a branch.
5. Select your main branch and /(root), then Save.
6. When GitHub Pages publishes the site, open the link and make your QR code point to that link.

The site works on a phone. Google Fonts load when internet is available; fallback fonts are included.
